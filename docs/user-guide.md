# User Guide

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Navigate to the registration page
2. Enter your full name, email, and password
3. Optionally provide your phone number
4. Click "Register"
5. You'll be logged in automatically and can start filing complaints

### Filing a Complaint

1. From the dashboard, click "File new complaint"
2. Fill in the complaint details:
   - **Title:** A brief subject for your complaint
   - **Description:** Detailed explanation of the issue
   - **Category:** Select the relevant category (Billing, Service Quality, etc.)
   - **Priority:** Suggest a priority level (affects response time)
   - **Attachments:** Upload supporting files (PNG, JPG, PDF; max 5MB each, 3 files total)
3. Review your entry and confirm
4. You'll receive a unique ticket number (e.g., CMP-2026-000123)
5. Keep this number to track your complaint

### Tracking Your Complaint

#### Dashboard
- View all your complaints and their current status
- See unread notifications
- Quick access to recent complaints

#### My Complaints
- Search complaints by ticket ID
- Filter by status (Submitted, Assigned, In Progress, etc.)
- Sort by creation date
- View pagination controls for easier browsing

#### Complaint Details
- View full complaint information
- See all attached files
- Read the complete history of actions taken
- View staff remarks and updates
- Track resolution timeline

### Understanding Complaint Status

- **SUBMITTED:** Complaint received and awaiting assignment
- **ASSIGNED:** Assigned to staff member for investigation
- **IN_PROGRESS:** Staff is actively working on the issue
- **RESOLVED:** Staff has provided a resolution
- **REOPENED:** You or admin reopened a resolved complaint
- **CLOSED:** Issue is finalized and closed

### Reopening a Complaint

If a resolved complaint doesn't address your issue:
1. Go to the complaint details
2. Click "Reopen Complaint"
3. Provide a reason for reopening
4. The complaint status changes back to REOPENED for further investigation

### Providing Feedback

After a complaint is resolved:
1. Go to the complaint details
2. Click "Give Feedback"
3. Rate the resolution (1-5 stars)
4. Optionally add comments about your experience
5. Submit the feedback

### Notifications

- **Notification Bell:** Shows unread notification count
- **Notifications Page:** View all notifications about your complaints
- Notifications appear when:
  - Your complaint is assigned
  - Status changes
  - Staff adds remarks
  - Complaint is resolved or closed

### Tips

- Save your ticket number in a safe place
- Check notifications regularly for updates
- Provide detailed descriptions in complaints
- Attach relevant files to support your case
- Respond to staff requests promptly if needed

## Troubleshooting

### Can't log in?
- Verify your email address is correct
- Reset your password if you forgot it
- Ensure CAPS LOCK is off
- Try clearing browser cookies

### Complaint not showing?
- Refresh the page
- Check if you're filtering by status
- Ensure you're logged in as the right user

### File upload failed?
- Check file size (max 5MB per file)
- Verify file type (PNG, JPG, PDF only)
- Try uploading fewer files
- Check your internet connection

## Contact Support

If you need help, contact the admin team through the system or via email.
